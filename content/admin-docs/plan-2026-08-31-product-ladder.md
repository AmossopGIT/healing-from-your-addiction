# Plan: payment ladder and 30-day commercial test

**Status:** Proposal for Gerald to confirm clinically and commercially. Prices are positioning ideas, not final.

**Related:** [31 Aug 2026 meeting](/admin/docs/meeting-2026-08-31-pricing-lead-nurture/)

## Verdict

**Yes — this model should convert more leads than asking for R12,000 from a Google ad.**

The mistake would be simply discounting the private programme. The stronger approach is a **product ladder**: people enter at a level they can afford, experience Gerald’s value, then progress when ready.

## Recommended payment ladder

| Stage | Offer | Suggested price | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Free | Articles, short videos, newsletter, WhatsApp tips | R0 | Capture and nurture everyone |
| First paid step | Wednesday online support session | **R350 once-off** | Convert a lead into a paying participant |
| Assessment | Private assessment and recovery plan | **R750–R950** | Understand the problem and recommend the right path |
| Membership | Recovery support group + resources | **R199/month** | Affordable recurring support |
| Guided group programme | Four-week structured group programme | **R1,500–R2,500** | Help people who cannot afford private treatment |
| Private programme | Eight private sessions | **R12,000 total** or **R3,000/week** | Gerald’s complete premium programme |
| Ongoing private support | Individual follow-up session | **R1,200–R1,500** | Maintenance or relapse intervention |

Gerald must confirm what is clinically appropriate and sustainable before locking prices.

## Funnel (launch first)

```text
Google ad or WhatsApp enquiry
        ↓
Free lead capture
        ↓
   Ready to pay?
   /     |      \
Not yet  |   Needs personal guidance
   ↓     |           ↓
Free     |    R750–R950 assessment
nurture  |
         ↓
   R350 group session
         ↓
R199 membership or group programme
         ↓
R12,000 private programme
```

## Why this works

1. **Removes the R12,000 shock** without devaluing the private programme.
2. **R350 already exists** and has good feedback — lowest-effort first paid offer.
3. **Creates a middle gap** (group programme / membership) between R350 and R12,000.
4. **Credits assessment toward treatment** — serious prospects feel progress, not sunk cost.
5. **Family / sponsor pay** — critical for gambling leads who may have no money left.
6. **Three choices after the form** — identifies willingness without rejecting anyone.

## Detailed offers

### 1. Make the R350 session the main entry offer

Instead of ads ending with “Cost: R12,000. Email us to begin,” lead with:

> Not ready for the complete programme?  
> Join Gerald’s confidential Wednesday online support session for R350.

Person should be able to:

1. Choose gambling or food / binge-eating support.
2. Select the next available session.
3. Pay R350 online.
4. Receive meeting link and reminders automatically.
5. Receive a follow-up message after the session.

### 2. Paid assessment before treatment

45–60 minute private assessment (~R750–R950). Gerald uses it to:

- Understand the addiction pattern.
- Assess urgency and suitability.
- Explain treatment paths.
- Produce a short personal recovery recommendation.
- Refer out when medical or specialist care is needed.

Incentive:

> If you join the full programme within seven days, your assessment fee is credited toward the programme.

### 3. Genuine monthly membership (R199)

Must deliver continuing value — not only automated messages. Possible inclusions:

- One online support-group session per month.
- Weekly recovery lesson or exercise.
- Short audio exercises from Gerald.
- Private progress check-in form.
- Member pricing for additional group sessions.
- Addiction-specific resources.
- Monthly “reset and recommit” session.

**Avoid “R5 per day” language** — too close to gambling micro-payment framing. Prefer **R199/month, cancel anytime**.

### 4. Structured group programme (middle offer)

Example: **Four-Week Gambling Recovery Group**

- One structured online session each week.
- Weekly exercises and urge tracking.
- Gambling-blocking and money-access plan.
- Support material and recordings where appropriate.
- Private check-in at beginning and end.
- Maximum 8–12 participants.
- Suggested test price: **R1,999 once-off** or **2 × R1,050**.

Similar track for emotional eating / binge-eating with clinical safeguards.

Ten people at R1,999 ≈ R19,990 revenue while each person pays far less than private treatment.

### 5. Keep R12,000 premium

Present as highest-support option:

> Eight private sessions over four weeks  
> R12,000 total  
> or four weekly payments of R3,000

Show the **total transparently**. Weekly amounts reduce shock but must not hide commitment.

Clear distinction:

- R350 = one support session.
- R199/month = ongoing community and resources.
- R1,999 = structured group intervention.
- R12,000 = intensive private programme.

### 6. Let family members pay

Especially for gambling leads. Partner, parent, sibling, employer, or friend may sponsor.

> Paying on behalf of someone you care about?

Payer receives payment documentation only — not confidential clinical information — unless the client consents.

Options: sponsor R350 session, assessment, first R3,000 instalment, full programme, or secure WhatsApp payment link.

### 7. Deposits for serious intent

> Reserve your consultation and programme place with a R500 or R1,000 deposit. The deposit is deducted from your programme fee.

Terms, refundability, expiry, and cancellation must be clear.

### 8. Three (or four) immediate choices after the form

Do not only say “We will contact you.” Show:

> What would help you most right now?

- I need someone to contact me
- Book the next R350 group session
- Book a private assessment
- I cannot afford help yet — send me free support

### 9. Fast WhatsApp follow-up

> Hi [Name], thank you for reaching out to Healing From Your Addiction. You don’t have to decide on the full programme today. You can begin with our R350 online support session, book a private assessment, or receive free resources. Which would help you most?

Suggested buttons: Join for R350 · Book assessment · Talk to Gerald · Free resources

### 10. Founding-member pilot (before complex subscriptions)

> Founding Recovery Membership  
> R199/month for the first three months  
> Includes monthly group support, weekly exercises and recovery resources. Cancel anytime.

Start with payment links and a simple member list. Measure accept rate, attendance, cancellations, engagement, progression to group/private, and Gerald’s workload.

## Best immediate commercial test (next 30 days)

Test **only four offers**:

1. Free resources for people who cannot pay.
2. R350 Wednesday group session.
3. R850 private assessment, credited toward treatment.
4. R12,000 private programme, payable as R3,000 weekly.

Introduce R199 membership and structured group programme **after** Gerald sees what people actually choose.

Manual payment links + admin notes are enough for the **30-day pilot**; automate booking and membership after demand is proven.

## Risks and safeguards

- Do not overpromise cure outcomes on lower-priced offers.
- Group and membership content must stay clinically appropriate for gambling and binge eating.
- Assessment must include clear referral-out path for medical / high-risk cases.
- Weekly instalments must show full R12,000 commitment.
- POPIA: sponsor payments must not expose clinical detail without consent.
