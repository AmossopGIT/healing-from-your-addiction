# After invite: start the course

Operational Week 1 launch guide for admins.

**Canonical guide (with PDF export):** [/admin/docs/after-invite-start-the-course/](/admin/docs/after-invite-start-the-course/)

Invite creates portal access only. After intake and consultation:

1. Open the client **Programme** page
2. Assign the **interactive** programme (focus-matched template)
3. Confirm sessions 1–2 are available **with client receipts**
4. Release week 1 guide when the gambling pack applies
5. Confirm the Tuesday/Friday live slot

See also: [Lead to client onboarding flow](./LEAD_TO_CLIENT_ONBOARDING_FLOW.md).
