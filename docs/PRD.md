# PRD - Healing From Your Addiction Lead Capture Website

## Product

Healing From Your Addiction is a South African addiction pattern support platform by Gerald Crawford. It offers confidential hypnotherapy, EFT, coaching-style support and educational resources for people struggling with addictive patterns.

## Goal

Build a fast, professional, SEO-focused lead capture website that converts Google Ads and organic search traffic into confidential enquiries.

## Initial focus

- Gambling addiction / betting addiction / online gambling support
- Food addiction / binge eating / emotional eating support

## Audience

Primary users are people in South Africa searching for confidential addiction help. Secondary users include family members, people exploring hypnotherapy, and people who want support but are not ready for rehab.

## Core journeys

1. User searches for gambling addiction help.
2. User lands on `/addictions/gambling-addiction-help/`.
3. Page matches the search intent, shows a form above the fold, builds trust and explains the programme.
4. User submits a form or clicks WhatsApp/email/phone.
5. Form redirects to a thank-you page such as `/thank-you/gambling-addiction/` for conversion tracking.

The same pattern applies to food addiction and future addiction pages.

## Required lead form fields

- Full name
- Email
- Phone / WhatsApp number
- Addiction concern
- Preferred contact method
- Message
- Consent checkbox: "I understand this is an enquiry and not emergency medical support."

## Success metrics

- Form submissions
- WhatsApp clicks
- Email clicks
- Phone clicks
- CTA clicks
- Landing page conversion rate
- Google Ads cost per lead
- Search term quality
- Organic impressions and clicks

## Compliance

Do not claim to cure addiction, guarantee results, replace medical detox, replace psychiatric care, replace emergency support or replace licensed rehabilitation where required.
