# HFYA | Search | Food Addiction | ZA

## Campaign Summary

- **Channel:** Search
- **Region:** South Africa
- **Primary goal:** Confidential lead enquiries
- **Landing page:** `https://healingfromyouraddiction.co.za/food-addiction-binge-eating-healing-program/`
- **Positioning:** Structured 8-session programme focused on binge eating, emotional eating, food cravings, body reconnection, mindful eating, trigger mastery, and long-term balance.

## Messaging Guardrail Alignment

- Use support-oriented language.
- Avoid cure, guaranteed, or instant-result claims.
- Avoid shaming language around food patterns.
- Keep wording calm, practical, and confidential.
