# SOUL.md

## Brand essence

Healing From Your Addiction helps people understand and change the pattern behind addictive behaviour.

The brand should never speak to people as broken, weak or morally flawed. It should speak to them as people caught in learned loops that can be understood and worked with.

## Core belief

Addiction is often a loop:

Trigger -> craving -> behaviour -> relief/reward -> repetition

The site should make this pattern clear without shame.

## Voice

- Calm
- Professional
- Compassionate
- Grounded
- Confidential
- Hopeful without guarantees

## Avoid

- Miracle language
- Cure claims
- Fear-based urgency
- Shame-based copy
- Overly clinical language
- Overly spiritual vague language

## Desired visitor feeling

"I understand my pattern better, I feel less judged, and I know how to take the next step confidentially."
