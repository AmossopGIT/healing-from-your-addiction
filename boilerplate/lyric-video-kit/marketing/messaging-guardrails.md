# Messaging guardrails (lyric video + social)

Portable copy rules for addiction-recovery content. Adapt `siteUrl` and programme details per project.

## Core standard

Support behavioural change without implying guaranteed outcomes.

## Prefer

- `support`, `structured programme`, `may help`, `focused on`, `aim is`
- Respectful, non-judgmental, confidential tone
- Pattern work and guidance — not medical cure claims

## Do not use

- `cure`, `guaranteed cure`, `permanent cure`, `instant fix`
- `100% success`, `guaranteed results`, `works for everyone`
- Fear-based or shame-based language

## Lyric video social copy

- Frame the song as reflection on patterns, not a promise of recovery.
- Link to the article for context; note when content is not emergency care.
- Match YouTube/TikTok descriptions to the article’s calm positioning.

## Pre-publish check

1. No guaranteed result claims.
2. No cure language.
3. No unverified medical promises.
4. Article link and site name correct.
