# Messaging guardrails (social video)

Short checklist for social video copy and hooks. For full programme rules, copy [lyric-video-kit messaging-guardrails.md](../../lyric-video-kit/marketing/messaging-guardrails.md) into your project.

## Do

- Use calm, pattern-focused language (loops, pauses, triggers, substitution)
- Link to the article for context
- Note when content is not emergency or medical detox care
- Match YouTube/TikTok descriptions to the article's positioning

## Do not

- Promise cures, guaranteed recovery, or instant results
- Make medical claims or replace clinical detox advice
- Use shame, crisis imagery, or stigmatizing labels in hooks
- Keyword-stuff hooks — write for humans first

## Hooks

- One clear question or observation per hook line
- Readable at phone size in the top safe band (see [platform-specs.md](./platform-specs.md))
- Timed 0–4 seconds typical; avoid covering sung lyrics in the cream band
